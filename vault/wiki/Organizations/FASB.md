# FASB

Companies objected to the removal of the option to use pooling-of-interests, so the Financial Accounting Standards Board removed amortization as a concession. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)#Amortization and adjustments to carrying value|§ Amortization and adjustments to carrying value]]) Private companies in the United States may elect to amortize goodwill over a period of ten years or less under an accounting alternative from the Private Company Council of the FASB. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)|line 14]])

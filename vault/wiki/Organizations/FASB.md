# FASB

FASB is the body that issues accounting standards, as shown by the fact that it issued FAS 142, which removed amortization of goodwill under U.S. GAAP. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)#Amortization and adjustments to carrying value|§ Amortization and adjustments to carrying value]]) The accounting for goodwill is rules-based, with rules that have changed and can change periodically, and the current rules are highly subjective. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)#Controversy|§ Controversy]])

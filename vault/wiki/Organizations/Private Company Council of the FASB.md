# Private Company Council of the FASB

The Private Company Council of the FASB allows private companies in the United States to elect to amortize goodwill over a period of ten years or less under an accounting alternative. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)|line 14]]) This council's actions relate to the removal of amortization as a concession by the FASB, which was done because companies objected to the removal of the option to use pooling-of-interests. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)#Amortization and adjustments to carrying value|§ Amortization and adjustments to carrying value]])

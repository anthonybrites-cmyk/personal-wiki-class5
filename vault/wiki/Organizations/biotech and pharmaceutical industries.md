# biotech and pharmaceutical industries

The biotech and pharmaceutical industries utilize contingent value rights (CVRs), which help bridge negotiations by allowing shareholders to receive additional cash, securities, or benefits if a specific, value-increasing event occurs within a set timeframe. ([[raw/Contingent value rights#Forms|§ Forms]]) CVRs are also common in this industry and are often granted to shareholders in companies undergoing significant, value-accretive restructuring.

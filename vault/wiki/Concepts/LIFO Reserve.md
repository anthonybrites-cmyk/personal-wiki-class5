# LIFO Reserve

The LIFO reserve is the difference between the cost of inventory calculated under the FIFO and LIFO methods. ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#LIFO|§ LIFO]]) This reserve is a contra account, representing the amount by which an entity's taxable income has been deferred by using the LIFO method. ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#LIFO|§ LIFO]])

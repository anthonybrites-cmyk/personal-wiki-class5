# Cost of Goods Sold

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is related to inventory costing methods like FIFO and LIFO. ([[raw/FIFO and LIFO accounting#FIFO|§ FIFO]]) Under FIFO, the cost of inventory reported on the balance sheet represents the cost of the inventory purchased earliest. ([[raw/FIFO and LIFO accounting#FIFO|§ FIFO]]) For example, if Foo Co. sells 210 units, the total cost of sales for November under FIFO would be $11,050, and the ending inventory would be valued at $5250. ([[raw/FIFO and LIFO accounting#FIFO|§ FIFO]])

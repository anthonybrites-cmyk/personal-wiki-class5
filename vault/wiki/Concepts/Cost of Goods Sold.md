# Cost of Goods Sold

If Foo Co. sells 210 units during November, the total cost of sales under FIFO would be $11,050. ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#FIFO|§ FIFO]]) Under LIFO, the total cost of sales for November would be $11,800. ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#LIFO|§ LIFO]]) FIFO will have a lower cost of goods sold (COGS) compared to LIFO in a period of rising prices, and therefore a higher gross profit. ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#FIFO Tax Implications|§ FIFO Tax Implications]])

# US GAAP

LIFO is used only in the United States, which is governed by the generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). ([[raw/Wikipedia - FIFO and LIFO accounting#LIFO|§ LIFO]]) Under U.S. GAAP and IFRS, goodwill is never amortized for public companies, because it is considered to have an indefinite useful life. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)|line 14]]) Since 2001, U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (FAS 141) no longer allows the pooling-of-interests method. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)#History and purchase vs. pooling-of-interests|§ History and purchase vs. pooling-of-interests]]) Goodwill is no longer amortized under U.S. GAAP (FAS 142). ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)#Amortization and adjustments to carrying value|§ Amortization and adjustments to carrying value]])

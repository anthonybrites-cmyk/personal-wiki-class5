# Intangible Asset

Goodwill is classified as an intangible asset on the balance sheet because it cannot be seen or touched. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)|line 12]]) Goodwill is a special type of intangible asset that represents the portion of a business's value not attributable to other income-producing assets, tangible or intangible. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)#Modern meaning|§ Modern meaning]]) Goodwill and intangible assets are usually listed as separate items on a company's balance sheet. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)#Modern meaning|§ Modern meaning]]) CVRs are common in the biotech and pharmaceutical industries (see Valuation of intangible assets).

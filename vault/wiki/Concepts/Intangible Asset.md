# Intangible Asset

Intangible assets are assets that are not tangible. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)#Modern meaning|§ Modern meaning]]) Goodwill is a special type of intangible asset representing the portion of a business's value not attributable to other income-producing assets. ([[raw/Goodwill (accounting)#Modern meaning|§ Modern meaning]]) Goodwill is recognized when a firm is purchased as a going concern and reflects the premium the buyer pays over the net value of its other assets.

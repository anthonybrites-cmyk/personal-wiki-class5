# Option Pricing

Option pricing involves applying a modified option pricing model to determine the value of contingent value rights (CVRs) based on the probability of an event, the time horizon, and payout rules. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Contingent value rights#Valuation|§ Valuation]]) CVRs are a form of option, with one type analogous to a call option where payout is triggered by an event, and another as a modified Asian option. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Contingent value rights#Valuation|§ Valuation]])

## Appears in

- [[Contingent Value Rights]] — an event-driven CVR works like a call option, a price-protection CVR like a modified Asian option

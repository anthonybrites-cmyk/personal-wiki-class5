# Acquisition Premium

Goodwill reflects the premium the buyer pays over the net value of its other assets. ([[raw/Wikipedia - Goodwill (accounting)|line 12]]) To acquire company B, company A paid $20 for net assets of $9, so goodwill would be $11 ($20 − $9).
